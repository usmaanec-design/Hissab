☁️ Google Drive Cloud Backup Assistance

If you experience any difficulties connecting your Google Account or uploading a backup:

  1. Ensure Active Internet Connection: While daily bookkeeping works 100% offline, connecting your Google Account and uploading an encrypted snapshot requires an internet connection.
  2. Grant Google Drive Permission: When prompted by Google, allow Hissab to manage its own configuration and backup files in Google Drive (drive.file scope).
  3. Save Your 16-Character Key: Your recovery key is generated locally on your device. Always save or copy it safely. Without this key, encrypted backups cannot be decrypted on a new device.

🐛 What to Include When Reporting an Issue

To help us resolve any technical issues swiftly, please mention the following in your WhatsApp or email:

  • Device Model & Operating System (e.g. Android 14, Samsung Galaxy S23, Chrome Web).
  • Hissab Application Version (e.g. v1.0.0).
  • Brief description of the action you were performing (e.g. "Exporting PDF report", "Restoring from Google Drive").
  • Any error message text shown on your screen.

Security Notice: Never send your 16-character recovery key or private financial figures in an email or message. Our team will never ask for your recovery key.
