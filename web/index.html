Private Cloud Backup

Transparent Google Drive Integration

Protect your financial records against lost phones or accidental uninstalls without surrendering your privacy.

How Hissab Uses Google Services:

  • 1. Your Own Google Account: You sign into your own Google Account. We do not use developer or central servers.
  • 2. Narrow Scope (drive.file): Hissab only requests permission to view and manage files that Hissab itself creates. It cannot read your personal photos, docs, or emails.
  • 3. Client-Side Encryption: Data is encrypted with AES-256 before leaving your phone.
  • 4. 16-Character Key: Your personal recovery key ensures only you can restore your books.

Restore Anywhere in Seconds

When you get a new mobile phone, simply install Hissab, sign into your Google Account, and tap Restore Cloud. Your complete books, historical ledger, customer balances, and settings are restored instantly.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, honest answers about Hissab, data privacy, and Google integration.

Is Hissab free to use? +
Yes, Hissab is completely free to download and use for personal and small business accounting. There are no paywalls on basic accounting features.
Does Hissab work offline without internet? +
Yes, 100%. Hissab is built from the ground up as an offline-first app. All your books, transactions, and customer balances are saved directly into your device's local SQLite database. Internet is only required if you choose to upload a backup to Google Drive.
Where is my cloud backup stored? +
Your backup is stored directly inside your own personal Google Drive under a dedicated "Hissab" folder. Hissab does not operate developer servers or store copies of your data on third-party servers.
Does Hissab see my Google password or financial numbers? +
No. Google Sign-In is handled entirely by official Google Identity Services; your password is never exposed to Hissab. Furthermore, before backup files are sent to your Drive, they are encrypted with AES-256 authenticated encryption using your private recovery key.
Can I restore my records on another phone? +
Yes. On your new phone, install Hissab, sign into the same Google Account, and tap "Restore Cloud". Enter your 16-character recovery key and all your books and transactions will be restored.
Is Google Drive backup mandatory? +
No, Google Drive backup is entirely optional. You can use Hissab indefinitely as a standalone, 100% offline accounting app without ever connecting a Google Account.
What happens if Google Drive backup fails? +
Your local data is completely safe and unaffected! If your internet drops or authentication expires, Hissab marks the backup as pending and preserves your local SQLite database without any interruption.
Can I manage multiple businesses or cashbooks separately? +
Yes. You can create unlimited distinct cashbooks (e.g. Shop, Grocery, Personal, Savings) with isolated transactions, separate balances, and customized colors/logos.
