Privacy Policy for Hissab

Last updated: September 27, 2026

At Hissab ("we", "our", or "the application"), accessible as an Android application and web application, the privacy of our users is paramount. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how it is used.

Core Principle: Hissab is an offline-first bookkeeping and cashbook application. All your accounting entries, customer balances, income, and expenses are saved directly on your local device storage. Cloud backup is entirely optional and under your direct control.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Hissab processes the following categories of information:

  • Financial & Transactional Data: Book names, accounts, parties/contacts, transactions, categories, and receipts that you enter into Hissab are stored locally in your device's isolated SQLite database.
  • Google Account Information: If you voluntarily connect your Google Account for cloud backups, we receive your Google Account stable identifier (sub), email address, and profile name. We do NOT receive your Google Account password.
  • Recovery Key: When cloud backup is enabled, an authenticated 16-character recovery key is generated. This key is used client-side to encrypt your data before sending it to Google Drive.

2. Google User Data & Drive API Scopes

Hissab accesses Google APIs solely to provide seamless cloud backup and cross-device restoration functionality:

  • Google Identity Services: Used to authenticate your account and ensure your backup is tied exclusively to your Google Account.
  • Google Drive Scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file):
    • We request the narrowest possible Drive scope.
    • Hissab ONLY creates, views, updates, and restores files created specifically by Hissab within your private Google Drive (in a dedicated Hissab/ backup folder).
    • Hissab CANNOT access, read, or modify any other files, folders, photos, or documents in your Google Drive.

3. Limited Use Requirements & Data Protection

Hissab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do NOT sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or financial data.
  • We do NOT use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • We do NOT permit human reading of your financial data or Google user data unless required by applicable law.
  • All cloud backups uploaded to your Google Drive are encrypted using AES-256 authenticated encryption (with PBKDF2 key derivation and HMAC integrity verification) before leaving your device.

4. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete any book, transaction, or all local data at any time from within the application or by clearing the app's local storage.
  • Cloud Backups: Your encrypted backup files reside in your personal Google Drive account. You can delete these backup archives at any time directly through Google Drive or by selecting "Disconnect" inside Hissab Settings.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke Hissab's access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Security Permissions.

5. Security of Your Data

We employ rigorous cryptographic standards to ensure your financial ledger remains private. Your backups cannot be decrypted without your 16-character recovery key and authorization from your authenticated Google Account.
